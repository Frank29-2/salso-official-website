About SALSO

The SAUT Law Society (SALSO) is a student organisation at St. Augustine University of Tanzania committed to promoting legal knowledge, academic excellence, leadership, professional development and unity among law students.

Through academic initiatives, community engagement and student activities, SALSO seeks to create an informed and responsible community of future legal professionals.
